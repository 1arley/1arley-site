# Home

Mode: Experience

Audience: hiring managers, recruiters, potential collaborators, and developers evaluating Arthur Iarley's real engineering depth.

Job: make the portfolio memorable immediately, then turn that attention into confidence through verifiable projects, roles, architecture, and direct contact.

Action: inspect real work first, then contact Arthur or continue to the full profile.

Constraints: preserve every factual claim, project, route, external link, bilingual behavior, monochrome brutalist identity, zero-radius geometry, accessibility baseline, and mobile fallbacks.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home is an interactive record sleeve crossed with an engineering dossier. It refuses the common portfolio sequence of centered introduction, equal project cards, repeated reveal animations, and decorative technical chrome. One authored opening establishes identity; every later section either proves the work or creates contrast for the next proof.

OWN-WORLD: Absolute grayscale, hard rules, sharp cropped imagery, condensed display type, readable neutral body text, and sparse monospaced metadata. Surfaces stay flat and structural. Orange is removed from the public home so hierarchy comes from luminance, scale, focus, and motion. Project imagery is treated as evidence, not decoration.

STORY: Visitors meet Arthur through a cinematic but readable first viewport, reach real projects before biography detail, then understand the person, stack, roles, backend discipline, and availability. The narrative moves from identity to proof, context, engineering depth, and a direct close.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-bleed black-and-white guitar image occupies the right and background while the name forms an oversized left-anchored typographic block. A compact navigation sits above it. The primary project action remains inside the initial viewport. Desktop motion links scroll to image crop and type displacement; mobile keeps the same composition without WebGL.

FORM: Brief-pinned Rockstar Monochrome, first choice by explicit user direction. Seed key: brief-pinned-rockstar-monochrome. Signature interaction: the hero behaves like a live album cover whose image and typography separate subtly with pointer and scroll, then lock back into an editorial grid.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
